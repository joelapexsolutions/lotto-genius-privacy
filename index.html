Privacy Policy - Lotto Genius

Effective Date: February 12, 2025

1. Introduction

Lotto Genius ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our Lotto Generator App ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data:

  • Personal Information:
    • For premium subscriptions, we collect email address and transaction details (processed securely through Google Play Billing)
    • No personal information is collected for free version users
  • Device Information:
    • Device type and OS version
    • IP address for analytics and app improvements
    • Basic usage statistics for app performance
  • Usage Data:
    • App interaction information (features used, session length)
    • Saved favorites and history (stored locally on device)
    • Generated number combinations (stored locally on device)
  • Advertising Data:
    • Anonymous data for relevant ad display (free version only)
    • Basic device advertising ID (can be reset or disabled in device settings)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • App functionality and performance improvements
  • Processing premium subscription purchases
  • Displaying advertisements (free version only)
  • Feature access management (premium features)
  • Compliance with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (for advertisements in free version)
  • Google Play Billing (for premium subscriptions)
  • Google Firebase (for analytics and crash reports)

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

  • All locally stored data (favorites, history) remains on your device
  • Premium subscription data is processed securely through Google Play
  • Industry-standard security measures for all server communications

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of targeted ads through device settings
  • Cancel premium subscription at any time

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

8. Free vs Premium Features

Free Version:

  • Includes advertisements

  • Limited local storage

Premium Version:

  • Ad-free experience
  • Full access to favorites system
  • Complete history tracking
  • Additional premium features
